# 🧠 Brain-Computer Interface(BCI) Website
A simple web platform to explore the fascinating world of **brain-computer interface**.This project combines theory and interactive simulation to demonstrate how brain signals can be translated into actions for machines .

## 🖥️Project Overview
This website introduces the concept of BCIs and allows users to experience a basic **Mind-to-Machine** simulation .Users can see how imagined brain activity could be captured and used to control a device .

## 🌐[live Website]()

### 🚀Features 
1. **Movement Selection:** Choose between left or right hand imagined movements.
2. **Signal Simulation:** Simulates brainwave acquisition using EEG signals .
3. **Mechanical Execution:** Processes the signals to control a simulated robotic arm .
4. **Interactive Learning:** Understand the BCI pipeline from signal detection to action .
5. **EEG Learning Page:** A Separate page explains EEG and brain signals in detail .



## 💡What is a BCI?
A **Brain-Computer Interface** is a system that allows direct communication between the brain and an external device. It can be used to help people with paralysis, control robotics, or even play games using thought.

## 🛠️ How to Use 
1. Open the website.
2. Select the movement you want to simulate (Left Hand or Right Hand).
3. Click **"Start EEG"** to begin the simulation.
4. Watch the robotic arm respond to your chosen movement.


## 📚Applications of BCIs
- Medical assistive technology for people with disabilities.
- Fatigue, stress, and sleep monitoring.
- Brain-controlled gaming and robotics.
- Research in neuroscience and cognitive science.
